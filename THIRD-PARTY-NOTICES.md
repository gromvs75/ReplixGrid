## Gridfinity compatibility mode

Gridfinity was created by Zack Freedman (Zachary Freedman) / Voidstar Lab LLC.
The mating dimensions and profile used by this mode are adapted from
[Gridfinity Rebuilt OpenSCAD, `src/core/standard.scad`](https://github.com/kennetek/gridfinity-rebuilt-openscad/blob/main/src/core/standard.scad),
by Kenneth Hodson. The TypeScript/Manifold solid construction is implemented
in ReplixGrid; OpenSCAD is not required at runtime.
